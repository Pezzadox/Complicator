# The Complicator privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The Complicator collects nothing about you.

## What stays on your phone

Your puzzles in progress, your streak and focus statistics, and your comfort settings are saved on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What is never collected

* No account, name or email address. There is no sign-in.
* No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
* No advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps or websites.
* No analytics and no crash reporting.

## Read aloud

If you switch on "read steps aloud", your phone's own built-in speech voice reads the step. The text does not leave your device.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
