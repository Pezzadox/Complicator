# The Complicator privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The Complicator itself collects nothing about you. The Android app shows one banner advert, supplied by Google, and that is the only part of the app that uses the internet. The iPhone app and the website have no adverts.

## What stays on your phone

Your puzzles in progress, your streak and focus statistics, and your comfort settings are saved on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

## What is never collected

* No account, name or email address. There is no sign-in.
* No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
* No analytics and no crash reporting.
* On iPhone and on the website: no advertising identifiers and no tracking.

## The banner advert (Android only)

The Android app is free and is paid for by one still banner advert at the bottom of the screen. There are no pop-up, video or full-screen adverts, and the banner is hidden in Focus view.

The banner is provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), and information about the advert you were shown and whether you tapped it. Google is responsible for that information; see [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where the law requires it (including the UK and the European Economic Area), the app asks for your choice before any advert loads, and you can change it at any time in the app under Make it yours → Advert choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. If you choose not to allow personalised adverts, the banner shows adverts that are not based on your activity.

## Read aloud

If you switch on "read steps aloud", your phone's own built-in speech voice reads the step. The text does not leave your device.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and itself collects no data from anyone. Adverts are limited to those rated suitable for general audiences. The Android app is not directed at children under 13.
