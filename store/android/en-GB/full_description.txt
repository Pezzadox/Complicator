A calculator that does the opposite of a calculator.

Type an easy sum, like 1 + 1, and The Complicator turns it into a chain of small steps that all land on the same answer. Work through every step to unlock it.

Built for neurodivergent brains: ADHD, autism, dyscalculia and more. A plain sum is boring, and boredom is where attention drops. A chain of small wins holds it.

BIG AND CLEAR
• Big chunky buttons, a big screen and high contrast
• A puzzle box in the corner shows the step you're on
• The dimension stack shows how far you've come: Chill is 2D, Tricky is 4D, Chaos is 8D

BUILT FOR ADHD
• Three puzzle tabs that each keep their progress, so you can jump between them
• Flow mode loads the next puzzle by itself, with a streak counter and focus timer
• Never stuck: a miss gets "have another go", then a hint, then the answer. No red crosses
• New way: a different route to the same answer
• Surprise: makes up a sum for you
• Focus view shows only the current step
• Break nudge after 15, 25 or 45 minutes, with a 5-minute break timer

COMFORTABLE
• Calm colours, bigger text and an easy-read font
• A gentle buzz on correct answers
• Steps read aloud

No account, no ads, no tracking. Works offline. Your progress stays on your phone.
